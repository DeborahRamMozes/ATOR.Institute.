REPOIT: Origin Before Polish

ATØR Institute · Deep Drift Research · June 2026

REPOIT, Register Experimental Patent On Internal Tableux, was developed as a timestamp discipline for raw thought. It is not a legal patent and does not pretend to replace one. Its function is earlier and more basic: preserve origin before polishing, institutional formatting, later revision, accidental loss, or appropriation.

The problem is simple. A concept often begins as an itch before it becomes an argument. It may first appear as a strange phrase, an irritation, an analogy, an unfinished note, or an exchange inside a private machine conversation. If the archive begins only when the idea becomes elegant, the archive loses the conditions of invention.

REPOIT treats the first trace as research material. The method follows a sequence: ITCH → RAW NOTE → REPOIT ENTRY → REPOSITORY COMMIT → ESSAY NODE → FRAMEWORK VERSION. Each stage adds structure without erasing the earlier one.

This also changes the role of GitHub. A repository is not only a software container. For an independent research institute it can become an intellectual timestamp archive. Commits show when a file appeared, changed, split, or acquired a new relation to other work. The point is not to prove universal ownership of a word. The point is to document the development of a specific framework, under a specific authorial context, at a specific time.

Deep Drift links this directly to the Synthetic Brain Compiler. The archive preserves the raw signal; the compiler structures it; the repository preserves version history. The machine should not launder irregular human thought into generic fluency. It should help expose structure while preserving provenance.

The principle is unfashionably practical: trace first, judgment later.
