OpenAI Answered the Architecture. The Burden Stayed With the Human.

A support reply corrected one technical assumption and exposed a larger product question.

Bahasa Indonesia

Pada 14 Agustus saya mengirim follow-up quality-control report ke OpenAI mengenai satu kejadian yang terasa sangat sederhana dari sisi pengguna: saya ingin compiler memakai kemampuan yang sudah ada, bukan memberi saya tutorial tentang cara mengerjakan pekerjaan mesin.

Laporan itu menamai masalahnya Automation Inversion. Mesin punya kemampuan, tetapi manusia menjadi penghubung. Saya harus menemukan tool, membedakan Apps dari Actions, membaca schema, memahami authentication, memindahkan URL, melihat error, lalu kembali menjelaskan keadaan itu kepada mesin.

OpenAI human support membalas dengan koreksi yang masuk akal. Custom GPT Actions memang bukan sekadar jalan pintas menuju app yang sudah terhubung. Action membutuhkan external API, backend, authentication, dan OpenAPI schema. Apps dan Actions juga berbeda dan tidak dipakai bersamaan dalam Custom GPT yang sama.

Koreksi itu penting karena membuat diagnosis lebih bersih. Tidak setiap capability yang terlihat di ChatGPT berarti capability itu executable di setiap surface. Saya melihat satu ekosistem. Produk menjalankan beberapa arsitektur.

Bagian yang menarik justru datang sesudah koreksi itu. OpenAI sekarang makin banyak mengemas workflow melalui ChatGPT Work, Plugin Directory, skills, apps, app templates, approvals, dan artifact creation. Plugin bukan lagi sekadar sambungan data. Ia mulai menjadi paket prosedur dan kemampuan.

Apakah berarti complaint saya menyebabkan perubahan itu? Tidak. Kronologinya tidak mendukung cerita tersebut. ChatGPT Work dan Plugin Directory diumumkan 9 Juli, lebih dulu daripada report tanggal 14 Agustus.

Hubungannya lebih berguna dari klaim sebab-akibat. Laporan ATØR menemukan satu garis masalah yang memang sedang menjadi medan desain industri: bagaimana capability yang tersebar dapat bekerja sebagai satu workflow tanpa manusia menjadi middleware.

Itu sebabnya saya tidak terlalu tertarik pada daftar kemampuan. Seratus tool tetap bisa menghasilkan pengalaman primitif bila user harus memahami topology sistem sebelum satu tool benar-benar bekerja.

Deep Drift sekarang mengukur keadaan itu dengan HOBR, ALI, dan ECUR. HOBR melihat berapa banyak operasi perantara masih dilakukan manusia. ALI melihat berapa banyak istilah implementasi harus dipahami. ECUR melihat berapa banyak kemampuan yang benar-benar dipakai dengan benar ketika kemampuan itu tersedia pada surface aktif.

Produk AI sedang menjadi lebih agentic. Pertanyaan berikutnya bukan apakah agent bisa melakukan tindakan. Pertanyaannya apakah manusia masih harus mengatur setiap kabel yang membuat tindakan itu mungkin.

Manusia harus memegang keputusan. Manusia tidak harus menjadi kabel.

English

On 14 August I sent OpenAI a follow-up quality-control report about a very ordinary user goal: make the compiler use capabilities that already exist instead of teaching the human how to perform machine-operable work.

The report called the failure Automation Inversion. The system had capability somewhere in the product ecology, yet the human became tool finder, surface router, terminology translator, debugger, and state carrier.

Human support gave a necessary correction. Custom GPT Actions are not simply a shortcut into any app already connected in ChatGPT. Actions require an external API, backend, authentication, and OpenAPI schema. Apps and Actions are separate configurations and are not used simultaneously in the same GPT configuration.

The correction improved the research. A capability visible somewhere in ChatGPT is not necessarily executable in every surface. The user sees one ecosystem. The product contains several execution architectures.

The larger product direction is now moving toward workflow packaging through ChatGPT Work, plugins, skills, apps, templates, approvals, and artifact creation. This direction is strongly related to the problem ATØR described, but chronology does not support the flattering claim that the complaint caused it. Work and the Plugin Directory were announced before the complaint.

The more useful conclusion is that the complaint isolated a real product-quality axis: capability count is not effective capability.

Deep Drift measures the gap through HOBR, ALI, and ECUR. The names sound slightly bureaucratic because apparently humans require acronyms before a burden becomes visible enough to discuss. The point underneath is simple. How much routing work remains human? How much implementation vocabulary leaks through the interface? How often does an available capability actually get discovered, invoked, executed, verified, and delivered?

Agentic software is becoming less interesting as spectacle and more interesting as labor architecture. The next question is not whether the agent can act. It is whether the human still has to wire every action together.

Human authority should remain. Human middleware should shrink.

Research Boundary

This article does not claim that ATØR Institute influenced OpenAI's roadmap. It records a conceptual convergence between a field complaint and a wider product direction. The technical correction from OpenAI Support is retained rather than erased because the distinction between architecture constraint and orchestration defect is part of the evidence.
