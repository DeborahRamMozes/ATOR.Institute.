THE HUMAN BECAME THE ROUTER

Automation Inversion, the Apps/Actions boundary, and the strange condition in which an intelligent machine still requires a human to carry its workflow from room to room.

Status: Working Black Paper. Evidence-backed critique. No claim is made that ATØR Institute caused any OpenAI roadmap change.

I. Bahasa Indonesia

Keluhan kepada OpenAI pada 14 Agustus 2026 tidak lahir dari satu jawaban bodoh. Jawaban bodoh masih sederhana. Manusia melihat salah, membetulkan, selesai. Perkara yang lebih buruk muncul ketika kemampuan sebenarnya ada, tetapi manusia tetap harus mencarinya, memilih jalurnya, memahami istilah internalnya, memindahkan state, membuka service lain, menyalin URL, membaca error, lalu menjelaskan kembali keadaan sistem kepada mesin yang seharusnya membantu mengurangi pekerjaan itu.

ATØR menamai keadaan tersebut Automation Inversion. Mesin menjadi penasihat. Manusia menjadi runtime.

Laporan ATOR-QC-OPENAI-2026-002 mengajukan tiga ukuran yang lahir dari keadaan itu. HOBR mengukur berapa banyak operasi perantara yang masih harus dilakukan manusia. ALI mengukur berapa banyak istilah implementasi yang harus dipahami sebelum tujuan biasa dapat diselesaikan. ECUR mengukur apakah kemampuan yang tersedia sungguh dipakai untuk menyelesaikan tugas, bukan sekadar tampil sebagai kemampuan yang katanya ada.

Balasan human support OpenAI memberi satu koreksi penting. Apps dan Actions bukan dua nama untuk benda yang sama. GPT Actions menghubungkan Custom GPT dengan external API yang memang harus mempunyai backend, authentication, dan OpenAPI schema. App yang terhubung di ChatGPT tidak otomatis berubah menjadi backend untuk Custom Action. Custom GPT juga tidak memakai Apps dan Actions sekaligus dalam konfigurasi yang sama.

Koreksi itu perlu diterima. Riset yang menolak koreksi hanya sedang membela ego dengan kostum metodologi.

Koreksi itu juga membuat problem menjadi lebih menarik. Sebagian kerja manusia yang sebelumnya terlihat seperti tool-discovery failure ternyata berasal dari architecture boundary yang memang dirancang demikian. Mesin tidak gagal karena lupa bahawa Supabase ada. Pada sebagian jalur, Supabase memang berada di ruang yang berbeda dari Custom Action yang sedang aktif.

Masalah pengguna kemudian menjadi lebih tajam: mengapa manusia harus mengetahui peta gedung sebelum dapat meminta mesin pergi ke kamar yang benar?

Bahasa produk menyebut Apps, Actions, Plugins, Skills, Work, backend, API, authentication, schema, permissions, approval, runtime. Semua istilah itu mempunyai fungsi teknis. Tidak satu pun harus dibuang hanya agar produk terasa ramah. Masalah muncul ketika istilah internal berubah menjadi syarat masuk bagi manusia yang hanya membawa tujuan kerja.

OpenAI sekarang bergerak ke arah workflow packaging. ChatGPT Work bekerja lintas connected apps dan files. Plugin Directory mengemas skills, apps, dan app templates. Dokumentasi plugin menyatakan bahawa beberapa kemampuan dapat dibawa bersama agar pekerjaan berulang tidak memerlukan perpindahan manual antartool. Arah ini berhubungan sangat dekat dengan problem HOBR dan ALI.

Hubungan dekat bukan bukti pengaruh. ChatGPT Work dan Plugin Directory diumumkan 9 Juli 2026, lebih dahulu daripada complaint ATØR tanggal 14 Agustus. Kronologi membunuh cerita yang terlalu enak. Tidak ada dasar untuk berkata bahawa OpenAI membuat perubahan itu karena laporan ini.

Temuan yang lebih berguna justru ini: ATØR menemukan garis masalah yang memang sedang dikerjakan oleh industri. Surface ingin menjadi satu. Runtime masih terpisah. Workflow ingin terasa seperti satu mesin. Di bawahnya masih ada Apps, Actions, permissions, external APIs, schemas, backends, dan batas produk yang tidak saling melebur hanya karena icon-nya duduk berdekatan.

Platform convergence dapat mengurangi beban manusia bila sistem mengerjakan lima perkara sebelum memberi tutorial: mengenali tujuan, melihat kemampuan yang tersedia pada surface aktif, memeriksa izin, memilih jalur yang sah, lalu menjalankannya. Tutorial harus datang setelah jalur eksekusi habis, bukan sebelum mesin mencoba bekerja.

Black Paper ini tidak meminta teknologi menyembunyikan semua mekanisme. Kendali teknis harus tetap terbuka bagi orang yang memerlukannya. Tuntutannya lebih sederhana: arsitektur tidak boleh bocor menjadi pekerjaan wajib hanya karena pengguna bukan engineer.

Manusia harus tetap memegang keputusan. Manusia tidak perlu menjadi kabel.

II. English

The OpenAI complaint of 14 August 2026 was not about one foolish answer. A foolish answer is almost merciful. The user sees the error, corrects it, and moves on. The harder failure appears when capability exists somewhere in the system, yet the human must discover it, identify the correct surface, interpret internal terminology, move state between products, inspect errors, and explain the machine's own topology back to the machine.

ATØR called this condition Automation Inversion. The machine becomes adviser. The human becomes runtime.

ATOR-QC-OPENAI-2026-002 proposed three measurements. HOBR measures the proportion of intermediate operations still carried by the human. ALI measures the implementation vocabulary a user must understand before reaching an ordinary goal. ECUR asks whether an available capability is actually discovered, invoked, executed, verified, and delivered.

OpenAI human support supplied a necessary correction. Apps and Actions are not interchangeable names. GPT Actions connect a Custom GPT to an external API and require the API details, authentication, and an OpenAPI schema. An app connected elsewhere in ChatGPT does not automatically become the backend for a Custom Action. A GPT presently uses Apps or Actions, not both at once.

That correction matters. A research program that refuses correction is merely vanity wearing laboratory gloves.

The correction also sharpens the actual problem. Some labor first interpreted as tool-discovery failure was instead produced by a valid architectural boundary. The user saw one AI product ecology. The runtime saw several non-equivalent execution surfaces.

The proper question therefore becomes: why must the human understand the building plan before asking the machine to enter the correct room?

OpenAI's product direction increasingly attempts to package workflows above these divisions. ChatGPT Work can operate across connected apps and files and produce finished artifacts. The Plugin Directory can package skills, apps, and app templates. OpenAI's own plugin documentation describes a workflow layer intended to reduce manual switching among separate tools.

This direction is strongly related to the research problem. It is not evidence that ATØR caused the direction. Work and the Plugin Directory were announced on 9 July 2026, before the 14 August complaint. Chronology forbids the flattering story.

The more defensible finding is more interesting. ATØR independently isolated a product-quality axis that the platform itself is increasingly forced to confront: capability count is not effective capability.

A unified interface does not imply a unified runtime. A plugin listing does not imply permission. A visible app does not imply execution inside every GPT configuration. A schema does not create a backend. An approval setting does not create a capability. A model may understand all these distinctions perfectly while leaving the human to perform the transitions.

This is why intelligence-in-action must be measured across the whole chain:

intent
→ capability discovery
→ active-surface eligibility
→ permission
→ routing
→ invocation
→ execution
→ verification
→ delivery

Failure at one layer can make an advanced model behave like an unusually articulate instruction manual.

The research demand is not that engineering controls disappear. Engineers need exact controls. Power users may want them. The demand is that technical architecture should not become compulsory human labor merely because the user arrived with domain intelligence rather than software-engineering vocabulary.

The human should retain authority. The human should not have to become the cable.

Evidence Discipline

Supported: the ATØR complaint addressed tool discovery, orchestration burden, abstraction leakage, execution substitution, and error presentation. OpenAI human support documented those broader observations as product feedback and clarified the Apps/Actions architecture. Current OpenAI documentation still preserves the Apps/Actions distinction. OpenAI now also packages apps, skills, and workflow guidance through plugins and Work.

Not supported: any assertion that the complaint caused ChatGPT Work, Plugin Directory, or later workflow features. Major convergence features predate the complaint.

Working hypothesis: workflow packaging will reduce HOBR and ALI only when capability discovery and active-surface introspection occur before manual-instruction fallback.
