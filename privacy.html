Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

ATOR-AI is an archival operations assistant for ATOR Institute. It helps users organize notes, research fragments, manuscript drafts, dialogue, archive nodes, and document workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when people use ATOR-AI.

Information users provide

ATOR-AI may process text, files, prompts, notes, manuscript material, chat logs, research fragments, and other content submitted by the user in order to provide archival, formatting, organizational, citation-mapping, and document-preparation functions.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to:

  • organize research and manuscript material;
  • structure archive nodes and document workflows;
  • generate draft formatting, export-ready text, and operational records;
  • support user-requested archival and productivity tasks.

Data sharing

ATOR-AI does not sell user content. User-submitted content is processed only as needed to provide the requested app functionality and to operate, maintain, and improve the app experience.

Sensitive information

Users should not submit confidential, legally restricted, private, or sensitive personal information unless they have the right and authority to process that information through the app.

Third-party services

ATOR-AI may operate through platforms, hosting providers, APIs, or app infrastructure needed to deliver the service. Those providers may process data according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data retention

ATOR-AI aims to retain only the information needed to provide its functions, maintain records requested by the user, or satisfy operational, legal, and security requirements.

User control

Users may request correction or deletion of information associated with ATOR-AI by using the contact method below.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ATOR Institute through the public contact page: ATOR Institute Contact.
