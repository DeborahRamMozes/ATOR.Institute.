# I. MELAJOE-HINDIA | STRATUM F

## 1. Perkara jang dilihat

`ChatGPT Sites` pada pengumuman OpenAI tanggal 20 Agustus 2026 memperoleh `Site co-editing`. Pemilik dapat memberi hak mengerdjakan situs kepada anggota lain dalam workspace jang sama. Editor dapat membatja `live database data`, mengadakan perobahan pada situs, menjimpan keadaan baru, dan menerbitkan perobahan sesudah pemilik lebih dahulu melakukan penerbitan jang pertama.

Pemilik tetap memegang urusan pembatja, aturan situs, analytics, hak milik, pemulihan keadaan terdahulu, dan pemberian atau pentjabutan hak editor. Susunan ini membagi pekerdjaan antara orang jang mempunjai kuasa atas situs dan orang jang diberi kuasa untuk mengerdjakannja.

`Editable Site URLs` memberi kemungkinan kepada pemilik untuk mengubah alamat ChatGPT-hosted tanpa membuat deployment baru. Alamat lama dialihkan kepada alamat baru. Barangnja dapat tetap satu, sedang tempat orang menemukannja berubah.

## 2. Observasi mentah

Situs jang dibuat dengan pertolongan mesin tidak lagi hanja merupakan hasil jang berhenti sesudah dibuat. Situs itu dapat masuk ke dalam kerdja bersama, dapat dibatja dari data jang masih hidup, dapat diubah oleh lebih dari satu orang, dapat disimpan dalam beberapa keadaan, dan dapat diterbitkan kembali.

Riwajat perobahan menjadi soal jang lebih berat daripada rupa achir. Satu halaman dapat kelihatan betul, tetapi asal perobahan belum tentu terang. Perobahan dapat datang dari pemilik, editor, perintah kepada mesin, data jang berubah sendiri karena sumbernja berubah, atau pemulihan keadaan situs jang lebih lama.

Alamat situs djuga bukan lagi satu-satunja tanda bagi diri barang itu. Alamat dapat berubah, tetapi situs dapat diteruskan. Pertanjaan lalu pindah dari alamat kepada riwajat barang dan tindak jang menjusunnja.

## 3. Tafsir sementara

Kerdja bersama pada `ChatGPT Sites` menambah lapisan pelaku. Kekuatan mesin bukan satu-satunja soal. Hak orang, kedudukan editor, keadaan data, urutan penerbitan, dan pemulihan keadaan terdahulu ikut menentukan apa jang achirnja dilihat pembatja.

Rupa situs dan keadaan data belum tentu mempunjai umur jang sama. Suatu keadaan situs dapat dipulihkan, sedang data jang dibatjanja mungkin telah berubah. Djika keadaan rupa kembali tetapi keadaan data tidak ikut kembali, maka pemulihan itu bukan pemulihan seluruh barang. Ia hanja memulihkan satu lapisan.

`Collaborative Artifact Provenance Fidelity` dipakai sebagai nama teknis 2026 untuk mengudji apakah asal-usul perobahan masih dapat ditelusuri sesudah situs melalui tangan pemilik, editor, mesin, data hidup, penjimapan keadaan, dan penerbitan kembali.

`Interface-State Divergence` dipakai sebagai nama teknis 2026 untuk keadaan apabila rupa situs jang dipulihkan dan keadaan data jang dibatja situs tidak kembali kepada waktu jang sama.

## 4. Hypothesis A

**H-A | Kerdja bersama menambah retak pada riwajat asal-usul.**

Djika satu situs dikerjakan oleh pemilik, editor, dan mesin, sedang situs itu djuga membatja data jang masih hidup, maka riwajat perobahan akan makin sukar dipulihkan apabila tiap tindak tidak membawa tanda pelaku, waktu, sumber data, keadaan situs, dan keadaan penerbitan.

**Ramalan:** sesudah beberapa putaran edit dan publish, seorang pemeriksa akan dapat melihat rupa achir tetapi tidak selalu dapat menetapkan siapa mengubah apa, dengan perintah mana, dan terhadap keadaan data jang mana.

**Bukti jang akan menguatkan:** log perobahan tidak membedakan edit langsung dari edit melalui mesin; keadaan data pada waktu edit tidak disimpan; pemulihan situs tidak memulihkan data jang berkaitan.

**Bukti jang akan melemahkan:** setiap tindak memiliki tanda pelaku, waktu, bentuk perintah, sumber, keadaan data, dan hubungan dengan keadaan situs jang dapat diperiksa kembali.

## 5. Hypothesis B

**H-B | Riwayat keadaan situs mungkin sudah tjukup.**

Kemungkinan lain ialah bahwa penjimapan keadaan dan hak pemulihan jang tersedia pada `ChatGPT Sites` sudah memadai untuk menjaga riwajat pekerdjaan, meskipun beberapa orang ikut mengubah situs.

**Ramalan:** pemeriksa dapat mengembalikan keadaan lama dan dengan terang melihat urutan perubahan, pelaku, serta hasil penerbitan tanpa perlu tjatatan tambahan di luar sistem.

**Failure condition:** H-B gugur apabila riwajat keadaan tidak membawa hubungan jang tjukup antara pelaku, perintah mesin, keadaan data, dan hasil jang diterbitkan.

## 6. Hypothesis C

**H-C | Bahaja utama mungkin berada pada data hidup, bukan pada banjaknja editor.**

Satu pemilik sadja dapat mengalami perbedaan antara rupa situs dan keadaan data apabila situs membatja `live database data`. Banjak editor mungkin menambah kesulitan, tetapi sumber retak jang lebih dasar dapat berada pada dua djenis waktu: waktu perubahan situs dan waktu perubahan data.

**Ramalan:** pemulihan keadaan situs lama masih dapat menampilkan data baru apabila database tidak ikut dipulihkan kepada keadaan jang sepadan.

**Failure condition:** H-C gugur apabila sistem memulihkan keadaan situs dan keadaan data sebagai satu kesatuan waktu jang dapat dibuktikan.

## 7. Mundane Rival

**Rival biasa | Ini mungkin bukan soal LLM.**

Kesulitan riwajat dapat timbul pada tiap sistem kerdja bersama jang mempunjai banjak editor, versioning, database hidup, dan penerbitan. Mesin bahasa mungkin hanja menambah satu pelaku baru pada masalah jang sudah ada dalam peranti lunak kolaboratif.

**Udji pembeda:** bandingkan situs AI dengan sistem non-LLM jang mempunjai banjak editor, data hidup, version history, dan publish cycle jang sama. Djika retak riwajat sama besar, maka sebab jang lebih kuat adalah susunan sistem kolaboratif, bukan LLM itu sendiri.

## 8. Kedudukan bukti

Bukti provider menjokong adanja kemampuan `Site co-editing`, hak editor untuk membatja `live database data`, menjimpan keadaan, dan menerbitkan perubahan sesudah penerbitan pertama oleh pemilik. Bukti provider djuga menjokong bahwa pemilik tetap memegang `version restoration`, editor access, analytics, ownership, dan audience control.

Bukti jang ada **belum** membuktikan bahwa provenance benar-benar hilang. Bukti jang ada **belum** membuktikan bahwa restore situs tidak memulihkan keadaan data. Dua perkara itu tetap merupakan working hypothesis jang harus diudji dengan percobaan langsung.

# II. ENGLISH HISTORICAL FUSION | STRATUM F

## 1. Object under examination

`ChatGPT Sites`, in the OpenAI release of 20 August 2026, acquired `Site co-editing`. The owner may invite another active member of the same workspace to act as editor. The editor may read `live database data`, alter the Site, save a new state, and publish later changes after the owner has made the first publication.

The owner retains the control of audience, Site settings, analytics, ownership, restoration of earlier states, and editor access. The division is therefore not merely between author and machine. It is also between owner, editor, machine-mediated act, live data, and published state.

`Editable Site URLs` permit the owner to change the ChatGPT-hosted address of an existing Site. The former address redirects to the new one. The public address may therefore change while the object is treated as the same Site.

## 2. Raw observation

The Site is no longer merely an output which comes to rest when first generated. It may become a common field of work, may read data which continue to change, may be altered by several persons, may preserve successive states, and may be published again.

The history of the act becomes more important than the final appearance. A page may be correct in appearance while the origin of a particular alteration remains uncertain. The alteration may proceed from the owner, an editor, an instruction given to the machine, a change in the underlying data, or the restoration of an earlier Site state.

The public address is likewise no longer sufficient as the identity of the artifact. An address may change whilst the Site continues. The question passes from address alone to the recoverable history of the object.

## 3. Provisional interpretation

Collaborative work introduces another actor into the chain of causation. The capacity of the model is not the sole matter. Authority, editorship, the condition of live data, the order of publication, and restoration all bear upon what is ultimately presented.

The visible state of a Site and the state of the data upon which it depends may not possess the same history. An earlier Site state might be restored whilst the underlying data have continued to change. If so, restoration of the visible layer would not amount to restoration of the whole artifact.

`Collaborative Artifact Provenance Fidelity` is retained as a contemporary 2026 technical label for the proposed test of whether the origin of alterations remains recoverable after passage through owner, editor, machine, live data, saved state, and publication.

`Interface-State Divergence` is retained as a contemporary 2026 technical label for the possible condition in which the restored visible Site state and the underlying data state no longer belong to the same moment.

## 4. Hypothesis A

**H-A | Collaboration increases fracture in the recoverable history of change.**

If owner, editor, and machine may all alter a Site which also reads live data, the history of change will become difficult to reconstruct unless each act retains actor, time, source, data state, Site state, and publication state.

**Prediction:** after several rounds of editing and publication, an examiner may recover the final appearance yet fail to establish who altered a particular element, by what instruction, and against which state of the data.

**Supporting indication:** edit history fails to distinguish direct human alteration from machine-mediated alteration; the corresponding data state is not preserved; Site restoration does not restore the relevant data state.

**Contradicting indication:** every act retains an inspectable connection between actor, time, instruction, source, data state, Site state, and publication.

## 5. Hypothesis B

**H-B | Version history may prove sufficient.**

A rival account is that the available saving and restoration of Site states already preserve a sufficient history of work, even where several persons take part.

**Prediction:** an examiner can restore an earlier state and plainly recover the succession of changes, the responsible actor, and the published result without maintaining a separate external record.

**Failure condition:** H-B fails if the saved history does not retain a sufficient relation among actor, machine instruction, data condition, and published state.

## 6. Hypothesis C

**H-C | The principal risk may lie in live data rather than in plurality of editors.**

A single owner might encounter divergence between the visible Site and the underlying data whenever the Site reads `live database data`. Several editors may enlarge the difficulty, but the more elementary fracture may arise from two distinct temporal orders: the time of the Site and the time of the data.

**Prediction:** restoration of an earlier Site state may still display later data if the database is not restored to the corresponding state.

**Failure condition:** H-C fails if the system restores Site state and data state as one demonstrably corresponding temporal unit.

## 7. Mundane rival

**Ordinary rival | The difficulty may not be peculiar to LLM systems.**

The same difficulty may arise in any collaborative software system which combines several editors, saved states, live data, and publication. The language model may merely add another actor to an older problem of collaborative computing.

**Discriminating test:** compare the AI-mediated Site with a non-LLM collaborative system having an equivalent editor structure, live data, version history, and publication cycle. If the provenance fracture is of the same order, the stronger cause lies in collaborative system architecture rather than in the LLM itself.

## 8. Evidentiary position

The provider documentation supports the existence of `Site co-editing`, editor access to `live database data`, saved states, later publication after the owner's first publication, and the owner's continuing control of restoration and access.

The available evidence does **not** establish that provenance is in fact lost. It does **not** establish that restoration of a Site fails to restore a corresponding data state. Those propositions remain working hypotheses and require direct experiment.
