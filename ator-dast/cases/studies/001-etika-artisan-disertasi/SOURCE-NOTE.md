# Source Note — Case Study 001

Case Study 001 was developed from the uploaded research manuscript `riset-etika-artisan-disertasi.md`.

The reviewed case study preserves the manuscript's central thesis while separating:

1. claims directly supported by primary institutional or legal sources;
2. comparative or interpretive analysis;
3. propositions that require further case-specific evidence before being used to accuse an individual or institution.

## Primary-source priority used in review

- ISI Surakarta / Pascasarjana ISI Surakarta
- Repository ISI Surakarta
- Peraturan BPK / official Indonesian legal sources
- College Art Association
- The Courtauld Institute of Art
- U.S. Copyright Office
- Direktorat Jenderal Kekayaan Intelektual (DJKI)

## Sources downgraded to background/context

Popular media, mirrors, Wikipedia, Medium, Substack, UX Collective, TheCollector, and similar secondary sources are not used as the sole basis for a legal or academic-misconduct conclusion.

## Review principle

**The heavier the allegation, the closer the evidence must be to the primary record.**

The original uploaded manuscript remains the source document for this reviewed case study. No claim in the reviewed version should be treated as a finding against a named person without a separate evidence file and proper institutional process.
