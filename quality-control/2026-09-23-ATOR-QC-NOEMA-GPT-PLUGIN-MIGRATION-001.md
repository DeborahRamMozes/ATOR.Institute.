**Quality-Control / Research Audit**  
**Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Subject:** NOEMA GPT-to-plugin migration and procedural-knowledge provenance  
**Status:** Open research question

## 1. Scope

Audit whether the observed GPT-to-plugin migration changes the operational, reproducibility, portability, inspectability, sharing, or provenance characteristics of a user-built compiler.

## 2. Source basis

Primary evidence is the preserved **NOEMA CONTRARIA — ITCH RECORD — From GPT / Compiler Friction to Plugin Migration**, dated 23 September 2026. The source explicitly distinguishes an observed product notice from unproven causal or motive claims.

## 3. Known

- Compiler and continuity problems were documented before the migration notice.
- The migration notice was observed on 23 September 2026.
- Skills/plugins are treated in the existing research frame as procedural software with identity, dependency, revision, permission, activation, synchronization, and provenance concerns.
- A mature compiler may contain accumulated procedural architecture beyond a single prompt.

## 4. Unknown

- Whether every compiler layer survives migration unchanged.
- Exact retention and access pathways for compiler content.
- Whether other users can access the migrated compiler.
- Whether functionality can be reconstructed from the migrated artifact.
- Whether any observed behavior constitutes unauthorized inspection, extraction, duplication, or redistribution.

## 5. Causality control

The following inference is prohibited without additional evidence:

**Earlier reports existed → therefore they caused the migration.**

Required evidence for causal attribution includes direct acknowledgment, documented product history, or another reliable causal record.
