## Claims Not Yet Strong Enough

| TIM failure is already causing major AI cluster downtime | Mechanism supported, incidence uncertain | Failure-analysis reports, warranty/return data, maintenance records, accelerated life tests. |

## Daily Source Discipline

1. Prefer official, peer-reviewed, and primary company disclosures.
2. Treat Reuters/FT/WSJ/Tom's Hardware as market signals unless backed by filings or direct company statements.
3. Separate country extraction from refining, separation, packaging, and manufacturing.
4. Separate model price from total cost of use: context length, output ratio, tool calls, storage, memory, agents, rate limits, and cloud lock-in.
5. Separate water withdrawal, water consumption, replenishment claims, indirect electricity water, construction water, and watershed stress.
6. Never let anger replace attribution. The ugly truth is stronger when each claim has a source ID.
