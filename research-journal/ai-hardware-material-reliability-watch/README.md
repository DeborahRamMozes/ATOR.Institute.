## Filing rule

This watch uses one file per calendar day:

```text
research-journal/ai-hardware-material-reliability-watch/
  README.md
  YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

Each daily file contains the observations, evidence ledger, hypotheses, source status, synthesis, provenance, and next tests for that day. Entries use real timestamps where known. Unknown time remains `TIME UNKNOWN`.

No separate `logs`, `daily-synthesis`, `evidence-registry`, `blackpapers`, `templates`, or `provenance` folder trees belong inside this research journal.

Editorial Black Papers and public essays remain outside the chronological journal. The journal may record that they were produced, but it does not duplicate them as a separate research-log taxonomy.

## Research object

This watch traces AI through the material system beneath the interface: copper, tantalum, rare earths, silicon, phosphorus, HBM, advanced packaging, TIM, leakage, electromigration, warpage, cooling, water, grids, data centers, price tiers, manufacturing concentration, maintenance labor, and writing-governance effects.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Peer-reviewed, official statistics, regulatory/government record |
| B | Company disclosure, standards or technical white paper |
| C | Reputable journalism / market reporting |
| D | Vendor claim or unverified trade signal |
| R | ATØR hypothesis or inference |

## Attribution rule

A software update does not automatically prove a mineral, water, energy, or local-community consequence. Material attribution requires a defensible causal chain and evidence at each link.
