## 3. Data Interpretation

No single observed LLM product update today proves direct additional extraction of copper, tantalum, neodymium, silicon, aluminum, steel, concrete, or water. Deep Drift must not invent direct mineral quantities from feature releases. That would be fake precision wearing a lab coat.

The correct causal chain to test is slower:

```text
feature expansion
→ user acquisition or task expansion
→ more active usage / background runs / agent fan-out
→ greater inference and storage demand
→ datacenter capacity planning
→ accelerator and server procurement
→ fabrication, packaging, logistics, cooling, grid, land
→ material extraction and ecological externalities
```

This chain is not automatic. It must be tested with timestamps, lag windows, provider capacity disclosures, datacenter filings, hardware procurement data, energy and water reports, grid interconnection queues, semiconductor supply data, and mineral price indicators.
