### Hypothesis register update

#### H1. Earth Capability Intensity Hypothesis

**Claim:** capability expansion tends to increase infrastructure intensity unless offset by hardware efficiency, software optimization, utilization gains, or substitution.  
**Status:** active hypothesis, not proven.  
**Rival:** infrastructure growth may reflect speculative buildout, non-LLM cloud demand, enterprise digitization, gaming, video, storage, or other sectors.  
**Failure condition:** usage-adjusted energy/hardware/material demand declines while capability and reliability rise.

#### H2. Price Capability Decoupling Hypothesis

**Claim:** subscription/API cost, segmentation, and feature gating may rise faster than practical reliability or user capability.  
**Status:** active hypothesis, not proven.  
**Rival:** pricing may reflect serving cost, compliance, licensing, support, inflation, storage, or bundling.  
**Failure condition:** transparent metrics show equal or better gains in useful-task reliability and cost efficiency.
