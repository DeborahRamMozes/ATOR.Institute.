## Status vocabulary

- **OPEN** — anomaly preserved; evidence collection active.
- **WEAKENED** — attractive hypothesis lost support but itch remains methodologically useful.
- **SUPPORTED** — evidence supports a defined hypothesis above alternatives, without necessarily proving causation.
- **FALSIFIED** — a prediction or causal hypothesis failed against evidence.
- **PROMOTED** — matured into a benchmark, formal Deep Drift construct, essay, protocol, or research program.
- **CLOSED / NO FINDING** — investigation produced no defensible finding worth promotion.

## Rule

No entry is promoted because the story is satisfying.

Promotion requires an evidence trail, counter-hypothesis pass, and explicit statement of what remains unknown.
