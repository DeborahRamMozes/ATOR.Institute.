## How ATØR grades claims

- **A** — primary source, official filing, direct technical document, standard, or strong dataset.
- **B** — reputable reporting, strong secondary analysis, or repeated signal with clear source trail.
- **C** — plausible but incomplete, anonymous market reporting, partial disclosure, or inference requiring caution.
- **D** — weak, speculative, unsupported, or not yet worth carrying as fact.
- **Rejected** — claim should not be repeated unless new evidence appears.

## What not to do

Do not quote ATØR as claiming more than the evidence supports.

For example:

- A financial guarantee does not prove final data-center construction.
- A chip price signal does not prove LLM subscription prices will rise tomorrow.
- A gigawatt campus disclosure does not automatically reveal water use.
- A model update does not prove a hidden conspiracy.
- A polished AI answer does not prove reliable cognition.

The archive is sharp because it refuses lazy certainty.
