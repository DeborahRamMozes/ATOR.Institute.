# ĀTØR Institute — Public Front Page

ĀTØR Institute is a research, authorship, and evidence architecture created by Deborah Ram Mozes / D-ORIGIN to study the hidden cost of intelligence: the human mind, the machine system, the material chain, the water chain, the labor chain, and the strange little theatre where everyone pretends technology arrives without extraction.

This repository is not a decoration shelf. It is an institutional evidence body.

## What this repository is for

ĀTØR Institute tracks how artificial intelligence, creative labor, infrastructure, academic writing, authorship, minerals, energy, water, and platform power become one connected system.

The central question is simple and ugly:

> What does the world have to sacrifice so that machine intelligence can appear effortless?

## Main research territories

- AI hardware materials: copper, tantalum, neodymium, silicon, phosphorus, HBM, packaging, cooling, thermal interface materials, leakage, electromigration, and hardware stress.
- Data-center infrastructure: water, power, land, cooling, local conflict, permitting, and resource concentration.
- LLM competition: compute access, model update cadence, pricing tiers, platform lock-in, and manufacturer pricing power.
- Authorship and writing economies: academic denial, AI-assisted writing, ghost labor, invisible correction, and human-machine co-authorship.
- Deep Drift / DSCC: Deborah-Style Compiler Construction, machine stewardship, continuity failure, benchmark design, and quality-control records.
- Art, criticism, and theory: RUPAISM, BMHS, Le Tissage, authorship ethics, and the refusal to let institutions bury the hand that works.

## How to read this archive

Do not read it like a normal software repository. That would be adorable, and also wrong.

Read it as:

```text
claim → evidence → uncertainty → counter-claim → next research question
```

A strong ATØR claim should show:

- what is being claimed;
- which source supports it;
- what evidence grade it receives;
- what cannot yet be proven;
- what should be watched next.

## Evidence discipline

ĀTØR separates:

- fact;
- source claim;
- inference;
- hypothesis;
- counter-hypothesis;
- speculation;
- unresolved unknown.

This distinction matters. Without it, research becomes perfume sprayed over panic.
